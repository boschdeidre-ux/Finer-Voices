## Gids vir die Onderwyser / Teacher's Guide

These worksheets are designed for **Grade 5 learners** studying Afrikaans as a First Additional Language in South Africa. All content is aligned to the **CAPS (Curriculum and Assessment Policy Statement)** for Languages – Afrikaans FAL, Intermediate Phase (Grades 4–6).

---

## CAPS Alignment

| CAPS Strand | Coverage in these worksheets |
|---|---|
| **Listening and Speaking** | Discussion prompts included in comprehension tasks |
| **Reading and Viewing** | Age-appropriate passages (~150–200 words); literal & inferential questions |
| **Writing and Presenting** | "Use the word in your own sentence" tasks; short written responses |
| **Language Structures and Conventions** | Vocabulary exercises cover synonyms, antonyms, and word meaning in context |

**Reading level:** Appropriate for learners aged 10–11 years.  
**Language of instruction in worksheets:** Afrikaans (with English teacher notes where indicated).

## How to Use These Worksheets

1. **Pair comprehension and vocabulary worksheets** on the same theme for best results — learners encounter key words in context before completing vocabulary exercises.
2. Each worksheet includes an **Antwoordsleutel (Answer Key)** at the bottom — fold or cut before distributing to learners.
3. Tasks are graded from **literal → inferential → creative**, scaffolding learner thinking.
4. The vocabulary worksheets can also be used as **pre-reading warm-ups** before the comprehension passage.
